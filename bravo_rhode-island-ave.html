A Few Words On

Breathing New Life Into a Historic Corridor

Rhode Island Avenue Main Street brings support, visibility, and resources to local enterprises — sparking economic growth and deepening the sense of community that’s always defined this stretch of Northeast DC. Marketing efforts, beautification projects, and community events work together to draw visitors and nurture a sustainable local economy.

Through partnerships with public and private partners alike, we equip businesses with real growth tools — financial planning workshops, facade improvement grants, and hands-on coaching — turning Rhode Island Avenue into a lively hub for shopping, dining, and entertainment.
