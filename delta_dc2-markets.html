DC² Markets

Good food, local goods and more reasons to gather — bringing neighborhood markets closer to home.

Why the market exists

Food access can feel like community.

In an effort to increase food accessibility in our neighborhoods, DC Squared works with community partners and neighbors to host neighborhood markets.

The next phase includes showcasing exhibition chefs to educate the broader community on produce preparation — and providing tasty samples.

Keep coming out to shop and help us grow.

The bigger picture

A market is more than a transaction.

It can be a place to meet a neighbor, discover a local maker, learn how to prepare something new, support a small business and feel more connected to the block.

That is the spirit behind DC² Markets.
