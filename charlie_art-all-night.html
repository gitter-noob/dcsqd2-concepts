✦A Few Words On

One Night, Every Storefront a Stage

Washington DC’s annual Art All Night tradition returns to Northeast DC each September, activating Rhode Island Avenue and Bladensburg Road with exhibitions, live performances, and family-friendly programming from dusk until, well, all night. Galleries, breweries, libraries, and neighborhood favorites all throw their doors open for one evening — no ticket required, just a reason to walk the corridor after dark.

Most recently held September 12, 2025 across both corridors, the tradition is DC²’s biggest single-night celebration of the artists, makers, and small businesses that give Northeast DC its character. Mark your calendar and stay tuned to our channels for next year’s date.
