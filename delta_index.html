Be Home / People First

Neighborhoods are built by people.

DC² is about the people behind the storefronts, the makers behind the art, the neighbors behind the events — and the everyday connections that make a corridor feel like home.

We support small businesses, promote local culture, improve food access and create opportunities for residents and visitors to participate in the life of Northeast DC.
